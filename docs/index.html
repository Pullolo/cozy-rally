Every stage is a new one

Each road, hill, tree and grandstand is grown from a seed, so there is always another stage to learn. Six biomes, day or night, rain or shine. The cars, the engine notes and the music are generated in code too: there isn't a single image or sound file in the game.

Your co-driver reads the road

Pace notes call every bend before you see it, like on a real stage. Here is what is waiting on the other side of the crest.

  1. Hairpins, chicanes, crests and jumps

    Procedural stages with real rally features, from Relaxed to Expert. Loops for laps, or long point-to-point stages.

  2. Gravel, tarmac, snow, ice, mud and sand

    Surfaces blend into each other mid-stage, and each one grips differently. Slicks love tarmac and kerbs, and hate everything else.

  3. Physics you can feel

    Suspension on every wheel, slip-based tyres, a clutch and limited-slip diffs. Assists go from Cozy (forgiving) to Sim (all off).

  4. Night stages

    You only see what your headlights reach. Flick to high beam, and try not to smash a lamp on the first tree.

  5. Circuits up to 20 km

    Club sprints, Grand Prix tracks and the Nordring: twenty kilometres of narrow forest road with blind crests and two carousels.

  6. AI rivals, split-screen, ghosts

    AI drivers who take proper racing lines, two players on one PC with gamepads, a campaign, and time trials against your own ghost.

Nine cars in the garage

Six rally cars for every surface, three circuit racers on slicks.

  • Pip

    Tiny front-drive hatch. Nimble, forgiving, a little slow.

  • Bramble

    Rear-drive coupe. Loves to slide. Hold that drift!

  • Thistle

    All-wheel-drive rally legend. Fast and planted everywhere.

  • Burrow

    Chunky 4x4 camper. Soft, bouncy, unstoppable off-road.

  • Blossom

    Pink turbo pocket rocket. Tiny, twitchy, loves to rotate.

  • Kestrel new

    Wide-body turbo rally hatch. Sharp turn-in, huge grip, a proper works car.

  • Comet

    Rear-engined GT racer with a big wing. Tarmac only!

  • Zephyr

    Hybrid hypercar. Silent shove, glued to the tarmac.

  • Dragonfly

    Open-wheel formula racer. Featherlight, all wing.

PC and phones on one grid

Up to four friends race online through a tiny server one of you runs. It has no graphics, barely uses the CPU and happily runs on the same PC you play on.

Server for Windows Server for Linux
  1. Start the serverRun it on any PC. It prints the address your friends should type in.
  2. Let friends reach itSame Wi-Fi works as is. Over the internet, use Tailscale or ZeroTier, or forward port 27420 (TCP and UDP).
  3. Meet in the lobbyIn the game: Online, type the address, Connect. The first player in picks the race, everyone picks a car, go.

How to install

Windows

  1. Download CozyRally-windows-x64.exe and run it. That's the whole game, nothing to unpack.
  2. The exe isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may stop it the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Plays with keyboard, mouse or a gamepad. Two players can share one PC in split-screen.
Download for Windows

Android

  1. Download CozyRally-android-arm64.apk on the phone and open it.
  2. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file app. Allow it, then tap Install.
  3. New versions install over the old one and keep your saves. Touch controls, or connect a gamepad.
