## What's new

**Slides are yours to catch.** On every assist level, from Cozy to Sim, a slide now behaves the same. Nothing countersteers for you any more: let go of the steering in a drift and the wheels point straight ahead, so you dial in the opposite lock yourself. No stability control, spin protection or traction control steps in once the car is sideways either. Assist levels now only set the steering range, traction control on wheelspin before a slide, and ABS. AI drivers keep their old helpers.

**Rally stages in Quick Race.** Rally has a new **Route** option: a loop raced over laps, or a **stage from A to B**, 3, 5 or 8 km long, against the AI or as a time trial with your own ghost. A small map on the track card shows what you'll get.

**Weather in Quick Race.** Pick it yourself: sunny, rain, storm, fog, snowfall, downpour, thick fog, black ice, changing weather or a storm brewing.

**Online race setup, reworked.**
- The leader picks the race on its own screen, with the big stage or track map and its elevation profile. The button says **Set race**: it sends the race to the lobby, and you start it from there.
- New rules: **Collisions** (contact or ghost cars), **Assists** (each player's own, or the same level for everyone) and **Car** (free choice, or everyone in the same car).
- Rally stages and weather work online too.
- The lobby shows the track and the rules in two clear lines.
