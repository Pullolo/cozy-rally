## What's new

**Steering keeps the front tyres gripping.** On Cozy, Normal and Sport, the steering again stops where the front tyres are at their grip limit, also when you turn in or flick from one corner into the next. In 0.5.0, steering against the way the front of the car was travelling could push the fronts far past their grip, so they scrubbed and the car ran wide. Slides work as in 0.5.0: once the front is already sliding, you can steer freely, and letting go still points the wheels straight ahead.

**Circuit cars need a careful right foot.** On Sport and Sim, flooring it at full lock out of a corner now lets the rear of the Comet, Zephyr and Dragonfly step out. Before, it never moved. On Sport the slide builds over about a second, so you have time to ease off or countersteer. Cozy, Normal and the AI drivers are unchanged.
