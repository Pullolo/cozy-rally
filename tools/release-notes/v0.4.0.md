## What's new

**Snowy Taiga.** A new biome in the far north: packed-snow roads between plowed snowbanks, and endless snow-laden spruces and tall Scots pines right behind them. The low winter sun gives a peach-pink sky and long lilac shadows. Also: frosted birches, red log cabins, frozen lakes, reindeer, and fans round campfires up on the banks. The snowbanks are real terrain, so you can lean on them in a slide. The road is packed snow with polished ice in the ruts and the shady corners.

**Taiga Rally, campaign chapter 6** (opens at 14 golds):
- **Spruce Corridor** (5 km): packed snow between walls of spruce.
- **Reindeer Crossing** (6.5 km): fast forest roads in the low sun, ice in the shade.
- **Northern Lights** (9 km): the long one, after dark, under the aurora.

Pick **Snowy Taiga** in Quick Race too, and online.

**Also:** the Linux server is now a static build that runs on any x86-64 distro.
